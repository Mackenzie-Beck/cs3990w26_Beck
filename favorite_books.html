Favorite Books

Here is a collection of some of my favorite books.


The Dispossessed by Ursula K. Le Guin

Easily my favorite novel. Le Guin has an excpetional talent for creating characters who feel rooted in alien worlds and allowing us to learn about these worlds through their eyes. I've re-read this book several times in my life and each time I find myself attracted to a different aspect of the story. When I was a teenager I found the 'ambiguous utopia' of Annares to be the most fascinating part of the story. As i've read the book again in my 20's I relate far more to the protagonist Shevek and his personal journey to find connection with his peers.

The Age of Surveillance Capitalism by Shoshana Zuboff

A fantastic analysis of the mode of power which has seemingly swept up the entire world in the 21st century. Zuboff goes into detail on the logic and structures behind the growing regime of surveilance capital, and the threat that this new order poses to human freedom.

Download from goodreads

The Social Instinct: How Cooperation Shaped the World by Nichola Raihani

A well written study of the evolutionary history of cooperation and competition. Raihani investigates this subject with a particular focus on how this history effects human behaviours.
